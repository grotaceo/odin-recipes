Cheeseburger

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 4 slices of cheddar cheese
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion, pickles (optional)
  • Ketchup, mustard, mayonnaise (optional)

Instructions

  1. Preheat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. Form the ground beef into 4 equal patties.
  3. Season the patties with salt and pepper.
  4. Cook the patties for about 4-5 minutes on each side, or until they reach your desired level of doneness.
  5. Place a slice of cheddar cheese on each patty during the last minute of cooking to melt.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  7. Assemble the burgers by placing the cooked patties on the buns and adding your desired toppings and condiments.
  8. Serve immediately and enjoy!
