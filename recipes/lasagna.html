Lasagna

Delicious layers of pasta, meat, and cheese!

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 jar marinara sauce
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated parmesan cheese

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook lasagna noodles according to package instructions.
  3. In a skillet, brown the ground beef and drain excess fat.
  4. Add marinara sauce to the beef and simmer for 10 minutes.
  5. In a baking dish, layer noodles, meat sauce, ricotta cheese, and mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers.
  6. Top with parmesan cheese.
  7. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for an additional 25 minutes until cheese is bubbly.
  8. Let it cool for 10 minutes before serving.
