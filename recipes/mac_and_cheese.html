Mac and Cheese

Creamy and delicious mac and cheese!

Ingredients

  • 1 lb elbow macaroni
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the macaroni according to the package instructions.
  2. While the macaroni is cooking, melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat.
  3. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Gradually add the heavy cream, whisking constantly to prevent lumps.
  5. Simmer for 2-3 minutes until the sauce thickens.
  6. Remove from heat and stir in the shredded cheddar cheese until melted.
  7. Drain the cooked macaroni and stir it into the cheese sauce.
  8. Serve immediately and enjoy!
